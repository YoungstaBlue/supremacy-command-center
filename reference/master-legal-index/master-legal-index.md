#### Standing Goal and Research Standard

- **id**: goal
- **type**: prose
- **paras**: Goal: precise precision and the best result in each court case. Everything in this volume is held to that standard.; Source order: official current text in force on the relevant date, then annotated statutes, then verified opinion text, then secondary sources. Case-file notes are leads, never authority.; Citator standard: confirm that each case exists, the quote, the pin cite, the parallel cite, and its later history and treatment. Say so whenever only existence and cited-by counts could be checked.; Form standard: start from the court’s own forms, Missouri Approved Instructions, federal AO forms and local-rule formatting, and list every deviation.; Also check: local rules, rule and session-law effective dates, weekend and holiday roll-over, and consistency between matters.

#### Claim and Defense Element Finder

- **id**: claims
- **type**: entries
- **intro**: Elements as generally stated in Missouri and federal law. Use these as a checklist, then pull the controlling case and the Missouri Approved Instructions (MAI) for the exact form.
**items**

##### Elements

- **j**: MO
- **k**: Conversion

(1) plaintiff owned or had the right to possess the property; (2) defendant took possession with intent to exercise control inconsistent with the owner’s rights; (3) owner deprived of the right to possess. Demand and refusal may be needed when possession was initially lawful.

- **chk**: yes

##### Elements

- **j**: MO
- **k**: Negligence
- **d**: Duty, breach, causation, damages.

##### Elements

- **j**: MO
- **k**: Bailment

Delivery of personal property for a purpose, acceptance, understanding that it be returned or disposed of as directed. Failure to return or wrongful release can support conversion or breach.

- **chk**: yes

##### Nine elements

- **j**: MO
- **k**: Fraud (Missouri)

Representation; falsity; materiality; speaker’s knowledge of falsity; intent that it be acted on; hearer’s ignorance of falsity; reliance; right to rely; proximate injury. Plead with particularity (Rule 55.15).

- **chk**: yes

##### Elements

- **j**: MO
- **k**: Civil conspiracy

Two or more persons; an unlawful objective or lawful objective by unlawful means; meeting of the minds; an overt act (tort) in furtherance; damages. Conspiracy is not a standalone tort without an underlying wrong.

- **chk**: yes

##### Elements

- **j**: MO
- **k**: Unjust enrichment
- **d**: Benefit conferred by plaintiff; defendant appreciated it; defendant accepted and retained it under circumstances that make retention unjust.

#### Glossary

- **id**: glossary
- **type**: entries
**items**

##### Affirmative defense

- **d**: A defense that admits the claim but avoids liability (limitations, release, privilege). Must be pleaded.

##### Bailment

- **d**: Delivery of personal property to another for a limited purpose with the duty to return or dispose of it as directed.

##### Change of judge

- **d**: Missouri procedure to replace the assigned judge, by right (Rule 51.05) or for cause.

##### Consent judgment

- **d**: Judgment entered on the parties’ agreement; appealability differs from a contested judgment.

##### Conversion

- **d**: Unauthorized assumption of ownership over another’s personal property.

##### Default (interlocutory order of default)

- **d**: Court entry that a party failed to plead or defend; judgment follows after proof.

##### Deemed admitted

- **d**: Matters in a request for admission treated as true after a missed deadline.

##### En banc / banc

- **d**: The full Missouri Supreme Court.

##### Heck bar

- **d**: Bar on § 1983 damages that would undermine a standing conviction.

##### In forma pauperis (IFP)

- **d**: Permission to proceed without paying court fees.

##### Interlocutory

- **d**: Not final; an interim order.

##### Monell claim

- **d**: Section 1983 claim against a local government based on its own policy or custom.

##### Motion in limine

- **d**: Pretrial request to exclude or limit evidence.

##### Nolle prosequi / dismissal without prejudice

- **d**: Prosecutor drops charges; refiling may be allowed within limits.

##### Qualified immunity

- **d**: Officials are immune from damages unless they violated clearly established law.

##### Qualified privilege

- **d**: Defense to defamation for good-faith statements to those with a duty or interest; lost by malice.

##### Return of service

- **d**: Sworn proof of who served whom, how, and when.

##### Respondeat superior

- **d**: Employer liability for an employee’s acts; not available under § 1983.

##### Statute of limitations

- **d**: Deadline to file. Tolling can pause it.

##### Tolling

- **d**: Legal pause of a limitation period.

##### Writ of prohibition / mandamus

- **d**: Extraordinary writs to control a lower court or compel a duty.

##### Sunshine request

- **d**: Missouri public records request under chapter 610.

##### Case.net

- **d**: Missouri Courts online docket system; some case types are login-gated.
