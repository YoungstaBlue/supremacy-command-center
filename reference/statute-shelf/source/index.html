    Welcome to the shelf

    Pick a volume from the shelf at left, or click a cover below. Volume I holds Missouri statute sections copied verbatim from the Revisor of Statutes; Volumes II–IV are working reference books — a dictionary, a thesaurus, and a Latin phrasebook — for reading and writing in a Missouri courtroom.

    Volume I

    Missouri Revised Statutes

    Verbatim section text, reproduced from the Missouri Revisor of Statutes. Each section carries its official citation, catchline, and legislative history note. Built to grow — more chapters can be added to this shelf as new matters open.

    Volume II

    Legal Dictionary

    Plain-English definitions of the terms most likely to come up in a Missouri civil, criminal, or family proceeding — the vocabulary of pleadings, hearings, and orders.

    Volume III

    Legal Thesaurus

    Related and near-synonymous legal terms grouped by concept, with a usage note wherever two words are close but not interchangeable — the difference between quashing a subpoena and vacating a judgment matters.

    Volume IV

    Latin Terms & Phrases

    The Latin maxims and phrases still in everyday use in Missouri courts, with a literal translation and how each is actually used.

    Volume V

    RSMo Title & Chapter Catalog

    Every title and chapter of the Missouri Revised Statutes, with the official Revisor of Statutes URL for each — the map to work from before copying any section text into Volume I. Section-level entries aren't cataloged here yet; add them chapter by chapter as a matter needs them.
