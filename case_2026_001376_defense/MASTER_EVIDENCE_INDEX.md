## 2. Exhibit detail

### Exhibit A — Sunshine Act incident report (7 pages) with original CAD dispatch log
- **Date on document:** February 2, 2026.
- **Source / how obtained:** Response to defense Sunshine Law request (RSMo ch. 610). Keep the request letter and the agency's transmittal with it; they authenticate the record as what the agency produced.
- **Contents:** Full 7-page incident report plus the original CAD dispatch log for the 01/27/2026 call.
- **Why it matters:** This is the complete record. It is the baseline against which Exhibit B is compared, and the CAD log in it carries the **15:10:40 EMS request for severe ankle pain** that supports Exhibit F4 and contradicts Exhibit C's denial of injury.
- **Foundation:** Records custodian or Sunshine response letter (business/public record). Pair with a certified copy if time allows.
- **File:** `02_POLICE_REPORTS_AND_CAD/EXHIBIT_A_2026-02-02_sunshine-incident-report_7pp_with-CAD.pdf`

### Exhibit B — Prosecutor disclosure report (4 pages), CAD omitted, "8-18-26 late burn"
- **Date on document:** September 29, 2026 (disclosure date).
- **Source / how obtained:** State's discovery production. Keep the discovery cover letter / receipt.
- **Contents:** 4-page version of the same incident report. CAD dispatch log is absent. Bears a notation of an 8-18-26 "late burn" (late BWC export/burn to disc).
- **Why it matters:** Page-for-page comparison with Exhibit A shows what was removed between 02/02/2026 and 09/29/2026. The late-burn note documents that BWC was exported almost seven months after the incident and after the Sunshine production. Supports Brady/Giglio demand, motion to compel the full CAD log, and a spoliation / adverse-inference argument on the BWC gap in F2.
- **To prepare:** Side-by-side redline of A vs. B (store in `02_POLICE_REPORTS_AND_CAD/working/A-vs-B-comparison.pdf`).
- **File:** `02_POLICE_REPORTS_AND_CAD/EXHIBIT_B_2026-09-29_prosecutor-disclosure-report_4pp_no-CAD.pdf`

### Exhibit C — Officer Jobe sworn Probable Cause Statement
- **Date:** January 27, 2026.
- **Contents:** Sworn PC statement. States no injury to defendant and does not mention any knee strike.
- **Why it matters:** Impeachment. Contradicted by (i) the CAD 15:10:40 EMS request for severe ankle pain (Exhibits A, F4), (ii) wheelchair use at the PD and hospital (F4), and (iii) the takedown and left knee kick visible in F3. Omission of the knee strike in a sworn statement is the core credibility issue.
- **Foundation:** Court file / certified copy; self-authenticating as a filed sworn statement.
- **File:** `02_POLICE_REPORTS_AND_CAD/EXHIBIT_C_2026-01-27_jobe-probable-cause-statement.pdf`

### Exhibit D — Hannah Griffin field witness statement
- **Date / time:** January 27, 2026 at 15:42.
- **Contents:** Field statement taken by officers; **not recorded** on BWC or audio.
- **Why it matters:** The statement was taken 24 minutes after BWC deactivation (F2, ~15:18) and after the on-camera witness coaching and "flail" language (F1, 15:16–15:17). The timing links the coaching to the content of the statement and explains why the statement was taken off camera. Use with F1 and F2 for cross-examination of Jobe and of Griffin.
- **Foundation:** Produced in discovery; authenticate through the officer who took it. Consider a defense interview/affidavit of Griffin (store under `03_WITNESS_STATEMENTS/defense/`).
- **File:** `03_WITNESS_STATEMENTS/EXHIBIT_D_2026-01-27_1542_hannah-griffin-field-statement.pdf`
