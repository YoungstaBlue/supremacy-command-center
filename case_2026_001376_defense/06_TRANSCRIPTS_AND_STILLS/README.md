# 06 — Transcripts and Stills

- Verbatim transcripts of each exhibit clip (F1–F4), with clock-time stamps every line, speaker labels, and `[inaudible]` / `[BWC audio cuts]` markers. Name as `TRANSCRIPT_F1_....md` etc.
- Frame stills extracted from the clips for the exhibit binder, named `STILL_F<n>_HHMMSS_<description>.png`. Extract stills with `ffmpeg -ss <offset> -i <clip> -frames:v 1 -q:v 1 <out.png>` and log the clip, offset and hash of the source clip in `stills_log.md`.
- Suggested stills: F1 "flail" utterance; F2 hand/foot signals in ground shadow and the cough immediately before deactivation; F3 left knee kick; F4 wheelchair at PD and at hospital, CAD 15:10:40 EMS request on screen if visible.
- Enhanced copies (brightness, zoom, slow-motion) must be labelled as enhanced and kept separate from the stream-copy clips; the un-enhanced clip is the admissible original.
