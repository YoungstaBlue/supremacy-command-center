**State of Missouri v. Tyler Ross Garner** — Case Nos. 2026-001376 / 2026-0032-01 / 2026-155, Circuit Court of Marion County, Missouri.

Place here, in filing order:

- Charging documents (complaint / information), probable cause statement as filed (see also Exhibit C in `02_POLICE_REPORTS_AND_CAD/`).

- Defense motions and supporting suggestions: motion to suppress, motion in limine, Brady/Giglio demand, discovery motions, motion to compel CAD logs and un-redacted report.

Naming convention: `YYYY-MM-DD_<party>_<document-title>.pdf` (e.g. `2026-10-17_DEF_motion-to-compel-cad-logs.pdf`).
